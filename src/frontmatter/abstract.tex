\markboth{Abstract}{Abstract}

This project presents a reproducible intrusion-detection prototype based on
IoT network-flow observations. The RT-IoT2022 dataset is verified,
deduplicated, and transformed under a versioned feature schema. A supervised
cascade combines a binary detector with a multiclass attack-family classifier.
Candidate models are compared using separate training, validation, and test
partitions, with probability calibration and controlled promotion of the
resulting artifacts.

The prototype includes a FastAPI service, durable and idempotent ingestion,
transactional persistence, post-commit real-time publication, and a React
interface for monitoring and investigation. A separate Suricata path preserves
signature-based alerts without presenting them as model predictions. The
system also exposes artifact versions, evaluation evidence, and model-health
indicators.

On the declared internal RT-IoT2022 test split, the complete cascade reaches a
macro-F1 score of 0.9520 for seed~42 and a three-seed mean of 0.9686. This result
does not demonstrate effectiveness on a real IoT network. The absence of a labelled native NFStream
corpus and of external evaluation remains the principal limitation of the
work.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Internet of Things, intrusion detection, network
flows, machine learning, RT-IoT2022, reproducibility.
